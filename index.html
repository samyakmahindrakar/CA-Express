About CA Express

Where Concepts Become Confidence

CA Prashant Taskar is a Chartered Accountant by profession but an educator by passion.

CA Prashant Taskar is fully dedicated towards coaching and education. He has over 10 years of experience in teaching CA students and has taught more than 1000 students over the past 10 years.

CA Express always focuses on concept building. The approach is to understand the difficulties students face while studying complex concepts and explain them in the simplest possible manner.

“Strong concepts create confident learners.”
